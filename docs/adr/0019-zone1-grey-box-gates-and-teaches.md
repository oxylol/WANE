# ADR-0019: Zone 1's grey box gates the techs a room can gate and teaches the rest

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-19
- BUILD.md sections: 5 (the twelve core tech interactions, "taught by the world, never by a tutorial pop-up"; "Taught by" means the cheapest route, not the only one), 3 (hidden rooms), 6 (Ember Toss 12 s cooldown), 15 (Movement checklist)
- Checklist lines: "Dash-jump keeps 100% of dash speed and clears the reference gap in zone 1 room 7"; "Pogo keeps full horizontal speed; a 10-bounce chain across the zone 1 spike pit is possible at 4 bounces per second"; "Pogo resets air dash; pogo, dash, pogo crosses the reference hidden-room gap"

## Summary

- Gated by controls (the only way across): rooms 02 dash-jump, 03 pogo, 07 coyote dash, 10 crouch-dash, 11 Ember-toss cancel, 12 Kindle-dash (see Decision 1).
- Gated except for a known cost: room 06 (no dash, no crossing, but a toss per crossing is not modelled) and room 05 (kicks beat the no-kick control; wall-jump plus up-dash is faster).
- Taught as the best way, not gated, because Phase 1 cannot: 01 dash-slash, 08 attack-cancel landing, 09 reverse pogo (see Decision 2).
- Room 04 (hidden alcove) is reachable by the Ember Toss waited out on a spike post: 2516 ticks against 7.5 s for pogo-dash (ADR-0016); the reward is no progress gate.
- Later content gates them: the boss with the short window (08), the shielded enemy (09), Snuffed in the pit (03), Snuffed beside wax (12); 01 has no gate yet (see Decision 3).
- The checklist's "zone 1 room 7" dash-jump gap is room 02 here; room 07 is the coyote dash (see Decision 4).
- Room 03 has two routes (rebuilt 2026-10-20 after the director's play: "The low roof is way too difficult and the lineup is almost impossible"). The main route is an open spike pit 2496 u wide under a roof 320 u or more above the spikes, crossed by a chain at the natural pace (8 bounces, one every 36 frames); its width is set by the strongest route without a pogo (2432 u past the edge: it falls 54 u short) and is sized against the roof (2505 u with open air above), and its 128 u deep entry pocket is 14 tiles so that a ground dash off the edge still lands in it. The optional path above it is the old low tunnel, 3008 u, with a Lantern-tier Votive marker past it: ten bounces at 4 a second cross it and nine fall 133 u short; the plain chain (4.6 a second) needs 11, the slow air-dash chain 6 (2.3 a second); a slow opening link (an air jump in the opener) lets nine cross, so the line is "ten are possible", not "fewer than ten fall short" (see Decision 5).
- Director questions are listed at the end (see Consequences).

## Context

BUILD.md section 5 says every tech is "taught by the world, never by a tutorial pop-up" and, after the review rounds, that "taught by" means the room makes the tech the cheapest route and a control fixture proves what single-use alternatives cannot do (ADR-0016). Twelve grey-box rooms in `content/rooms/zone1/` were reviewed one by one by fresh reviewers who tried to skip each tech. Several found routes that bypass the tech; fixers closed what geometry could close and wrote the rest into the room headers, the feel logs (`docs/feel_log/2026-10-19-zone1-*` and `*-room-*`) and `PROGRESS.md`. This ADR records the outcome room by room so the next phase knows which rooms to revisit when the missing content exists. The doc's older wording ("only a route with X crosses") was true of only some rooms.

## Decision

1. **Gated, proved by control fixtures.** Each control is a fixture in `tests/replay/zone1/` that spends every resource except the tech and fails to cross.
   - Room 02: the chasm is 1664 u, 62 u past the farthest route without a dash-jump (`tools/replay/gap_reach.gd`, held equal to `tests/replay/rooms/chasm.room` by `test_gap_reach_room.gd`).
   - Room 03: the optional tunnel's farthest route without a pogo ends 672 u into its 3008 u (`03_control_no_pogo`); a ceiling 64 u above the spikes forbids arcing over. Nine bounces at 4 a second end 133 u short (`zone1_03_control_nine_bounces`), ten cross (`zone1_03_pogo_chain_route`). The main pit is open air, 2496 u wide: the strongest route without a pogo falls 54 u short (`03_main_control_no_pogo_toss`), 216 u without the toss (`03_main_control_no_pogo`).
   - Room 07: chasm B has its lip at x 1604. A dash read on the last floor tick (93 or 94) crosses a lip at 1590 or nearer; a coyote dash crosses 1622 at the best phase and 1604 with 6 to 10 plans per phase. The first version put the lip 14 u too near and a plain ground dash on tick 94 crossed (review H); the control now dashes on the correct tick. This is a search, not a proof.
   - Room 10: the gaps are 32 u under a 44 u body and run floor to ceiling; a standing dash stops at the face, crouching alone slides 13.5 u. A toss skips one gap, there are two, and the toss is on a 12 s cooldown, so the second needs a crouch-dash or a 720 tick wait. That last is the cost, not a closure.
   - Room 11: the chasm is 2400 u and needs a reach past 2390 u; dash-jump with every resource reaches 2243 u, with one toss thrown mid-air-dash 2505 u (ADR-0017). Proven by hand on `test/longchasm`, not on the real room.
   - Room 12: the wax screens run floor to ceiling and the pit below the door has spikes; without the Kindle Votive both hold (`12_no_kindle_screen_holds`, `12_no_kindle_door_holds`).
   - Room 06: without the dash the farthest crossing meets the far wall 69 u too low (`06_control_no_dash_crossing`); one toss carries a no-dash crossing to band 1 and the landing trick to band 2 (`06_control_no_dash_toss`, 74 u short of band 3). A toss waited out on a band between crossings is not modelled (ADR-0016).
   - Room 05: the no-kick control (`05_control_no_kick`) tops out 128 u short, but a wall-jump plus up-dash cycle climbs the same well 85 ticks sooner (`05_wall_jump_dash_cycle`). The kick is the plain way, not the only one.
2. **Taught, not gated, because Phase 1 cannot gate it.**
   - Room 01 dash-slash: the corridor dummies are solid so a run or a dash stops at the first; the dash-slash chain takes 358 ticks, running slashes 434 (a 72 tick margin, about 7 ticks per dummy). Dash alone and run alone never cross (`zone1_01_control_dash_only`, `zone1_01_control_run`), but plain slashing does, so the tech is the best way, not the only one. Weak enemies that cannot be out-slashed arrive with zone 1's real enemies.
   - Room 08 attack-cancel landing: the ambushing unparryable swing costs a heart for every plain alternative (no strike, a strike on or after the landing, waiting, one dash), but two dashes outrun a 24 f telegraph and cross with 5 hearts in 174 f against the teaching route's 210 (`08_gap_two_dashes`, a known-gap fixture).
   - Room 09 reverse pogo: a run and one jump clears the 64 u guard (`09_note_plain_jump_crosses`); any jump high enough to strike its head clears it, so no geometry gates the strike, and a no-dash reverse pogo also works (2 ticks of window against the dash route's 6). The dash is the forgiving way in (ADR-0010).
   - Room 04 hidden alcove: reachable with the Ember Toss waited out on a spike post (`04_bypass_toss_wait`, 2516 ticks), with no air dash after the approach. Pogo-dash takes 7.5 s. ADR-0016 holds the reasoning.
3. **What later content gates.** The boss with the short window closes room 08's gap (its door cannot be crossed by two dashes; the gap fixture becomes a control). The shielded enemy closes room 09 (the unshielded back is the reason for the tech; the room changes when it exists). Snuffed in the pit makes room 03 a pogo line of enemies as written in BUILD.md; Snuffed beside wax does the same for room 12's Kindle-dash. Room 01 is gated when a pack of weak enemies that a plain attacker cannot clear in the corridor's time exists; nothing in Phase 1 does. The hidden room of room 04 is not a progress gate (its reward is a Lantern-tier Votive or note, ADR-0016 Decision 3), so its bypass needs no later closure.
4. **The reference dash-jump gap is room 02.** Checklist line "clears the reference gap in zone 1 room 7" was written when the list was ordered differently; in this grey box room 02 holds the dash-jump gap and room 07 is the coyote dash. The checklist line is satisfied by room 02 (`tools/replay/gap_reach.gd` on `test/longchasm`, `zone1_02_*` fixtures). BUILD.md keeps the wording; `PROGRESS.md` maps it.
5. **Room 03: an open pit for the main route, the 4-a-second tunnel as an optional path (rebuilt 2026-10-20).** The checklist line is a count and a rate in one chain; the first rebuild of the room put it on the only route, in a tunnel whose ceiling was 64 u above the spikes (a bounce rises 131 u in open air and lands 39 frames later, so the roof was what made 4 a second necessary and what made the lineup tight). The director played it: "The low roof is way too difficult and the lineup is almost impossible." He chose: the main route becomes an open pit with natural, relaxed bounces and an easy entry; the 4-a-second chain survives as an optional side path to a reward, so the line still holds in zone 1.
   - **Main route (west door to east door).** A hall (floor 672 u), the short patch of 3 spikes (tiles 9 to 11: a hop, or one down-strike on it, is the safe first encounter and the lesson), then the hall's edge at x 576 u and a pit of 78 tiles, 2496 u, with spikes on its floor and open air above it: the roof is the optional level's floor, 320 u or more over the spikes, more than twice the longest bounce (131 u) and clear of a bounce with its air jump (253 u). The entry lines itself up: the first 14 tiles of the pit (448 u) are 128 u deep, so a run off the edge falls about 17 frames to the spikes, and the down-strike's window is 10 frames (pressed 4 to 13 frames before the spikes touch: `03_main_route_late` at 4, `03_main_route` at 8, `03_main_route_early` at 12 bounce on the same rhythm); the spike line then rises to 64 u under the floor and its last 4 tiles to 32 u, so the last bounce has a lip. The pocket is 14 tiles, not 8, for the ground dash that room 02 teaches just before: a dash carries Ash 20 u/f for 9 ticks and then 10 u/f over the 17-tick fall, so from the edge he lands at x 918 u, and a pocket of 8 tiles (to x 832 u) ended in a step whose spikes he touched whatever he pressed (a dash 96 to 116 u before the edge had no surviving press, review F1); now every ground dash from 176 u before the edge to the edge has at least 8 bounce timings (the dash's own window; `03_main_dash_entry`, and the unit test sweeps the starts). The chain is 8 bounces at the run speed (9 u/f), one every 36 frames, 330 u, 1.6 a second, about 5 s from edge to hall (`zone1_03_main_route`); from six start points and the three press times every run crossed. A missed bounce costs a heart and the run back from where Ash last stood (`03_main_control_missed_bounce`). Why 2496 u and not fewer bounces: with open air above, width is the only thing that can stop a route without a pogo. `tools/replay/gap_reach.gd` (now with `--room`, `--edge` and `--ledge`, defaults unchanged) on `test/longpit`, the pit with no far side (held to the room by the unit test), finds 2432 u past the edge for the dash-jump with the air jump, the air dash and the Ember Toss (spawn 585, ground dash on tick 0, jump 6, air jump 50, up-right air dash 94, up-right toss 109, teleport 133), 2243 u without the toss, 1333 u for a carried jump, 1201 u for a running jump (the carry and run routes with a toss were stopped after 35 minutes; a toss adds about 260 u to a route, ADR-0017, far under 2432). A crossing needs the centre within half a body (10 u) of the far wall, so a pit narrower than 2443 u is crossed; 2496 u leaves the strongest route 54 u short. `03_main_control_no_pogo_toss` plays that route in the room and falls at x 3018 u (the wall at 3072 u); `03_main_control_no_pogo` plays the route without the toss and falls at x 2856 u. The width fixes the chain's length, not its pace; a roof low enough to shorten the pit is what the director rejected. The width is sized against the roof: on `test/longchasm` (ADR-0017), the same drop with open air above, the strongest route reaches 2505 u, and the upper level's floor 288 u over the hall takes about 73 u off it (2432 u on `test/longpit`); a crossing needs more than 2486 u, so the 54 u margin and the claim that no route crosses without the pogo rest on that roof (and on the search being a heuristic: its carry and run routes with a toss were stopped after 35 minutes). Opening the roof (a skylight, a well, a lower upper floor) would make the pit crossable, so it has to be widened with it; the unit test holds the roof row solid over every pit column. The ladder up to the optional path (below) stands 320 u west of the edge, so none of its platforms is a launch pad that adds reach; the unit test holds that.
   - **Optional path.** A ladder of three one-way platforms in a 96 u well (tiles 5 to 7, tops 576, 480 and 384 u; `03_optional_ladder`; `zone1_03_optional_ladder_chain` plays the ladder and then the ten-bounce chain without a pause: the ladder's exit at x 399 u leaves 36 u of run before the dash, and the tenth arc lands 242 u past the tunnel's end) rises from the main hall to the upper level (floor 320 u): a hall (the old room's, moved up 11 tiles), the low tunnel and a low hall past it. Reward: an entity marker for a Lantern-tier Votive at x 4160 u, past a 3-tile shaft (`03_optional_reward`), as room 04's alcove has one; the shaft is the way back, a 352 u drop to the main east hall beside the east door (`03_optional_way_back`). Missing the chain costs only a hazard (back to the upper hall's floor). Per ADR-0016 the room teaches and does not gate, and the reward is no progress gate: a player with the whole kit can climb the shaft from the main east hall (a jump, the air jump and an up air dash lift the feet to y 268 u, 404 u, over the upper floor's 320 u) and take the Votive without the chain; the chain is the way the room teaches and the line is held, not a lock. The tunnel, its fixtures and the arithmetic are the old room's, unchanged; the fixtures' spawns moved from y 608 to 320 and their tails now land on the low hall (`event.land`, x > 3584 u, y 320 u) instead of crossing the east door.
   - **The tunnel's arithmetic.** 20 u/f is the most speed Ash has (the dash-jump's, kept by every bounce, BUILD.md s5 Pogo); a bounce every 15 frames is 4.0 a second, 300 u a bounce; the first bounce lands 195.5 u inside the tunnel (x 771.5). The tunnel is 94 tiles, 3008 u (x 576 to 3584 u). Ten bounces at 4 a second (a down-strike on each fall and an air jump 5 frames after each bounce, which holds each link to 15 frames) reach x 3471.5 and the tenth arc ends 187 u past the end: `zone1_03_pogo_chain_route`, 10 bounces, 4.0 a second, vx 20 u/f kept at every bounce. Nine fall into the spikes at x 3451 u, 133 u short (`zone1_03_control_nine_bounces`); six then nothing fall at 2511 u (`zone1_03_control_short_chain`). What else is possible, measured: the plain chain (nothing between down-strikes) is 13 frames a bounce, 4.6 a second but 260 u a bounce, and takes 11 (`zone1_03_route_plain_chain`; ten fall 233 u short, `zone1_03_control_plain_ten`), so a faster rhythm is no shorter; a slower one is: an air dash and an air dash-jump between bounces make a link 26 frames and 520 u, and 6 bounces at 2.3 a second cross (`zone1_03_route_fewest_bounces`; five fall short, `zone1_03_control_five_slow`). A slow last arc likewise lets nine 4-a-second bounces cross (`zone1_03_route_slow_finisher`), and so does a slow first link, which needs no air dash: the no-pogo controls' dash-jump and its air jump (dash 44, jump 49, air jump 61) put the first bounce on frame 72 at x 1027.5 u, 256 u farther in than the route's 771.5, and nine bounces at 4 a second then cross with the ninth arc landing at x about 3745 u (`zone1_03_route_slow_opener`, found in review round 2; eight fall in). So the line is met as written (ten at 4 a second are possible, `zone1_03_pogo_chain_route`) and the tunnel asks ten of a chain whose links are all 15 frames from a first bounce on the dash-jump's own arc, not of every chain: a player who slows one link buys 220 to 256 u with it. The tunnel cannot be lengthened to close this: ten with the slow opening reach x 4027 and nine reach 3745, but ten from the route's opening reach only 3771, so a tunnel between the two (3170 to 3195 u) leaves the route 25 u of slack, and an air dash in the opener buys more again. `tests/unit/tools/test_zone1_room03_pit.gd` holds both routes to this arithmetic, reading the bounce frames, vx and first-bounce x out of the fixtures' own expectations. History: the first room had a 6208 u pit (17 or more bounces at 4 a second; the audit found the line unmet), the second the 3008 u tunnel as the only route (the director's play, above). `zone1_loop` is 3696 ticks (61.6 s): room 3 takes 456 (the main route, 9 bounces counting the patch); six passes are 22,176 ticks (6.2 minutes) for the perf gate.
6. **Headers are honest.** Every room header says which of these it is, names its controls and bypass fixtures, and says what is not modelled. A claim of "the only way" appears only where a control backs it.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Gate every tech with geometry no matter what | Rooms 01, 08 and 09 cannot be gated by static geometry and dummies: a dash outruns a telegraph, a jump over a 64 u guard clears it, a corridor of dummies is crossed by plain slashes. Tuning them to fake it makes them timing tests that no longer teach. |
| Add core rules to close bypasses (pause the toss cooldown off the ground, change the wall-jump restore) | Changes BUILD.md section 6 and the wall-jump row, and would break rooms 05 and 06 (ADR-0016). Not a room-design problem. |
| Tutorial pop-ups for the techs that cannot be gated | Forbidden by BUILD.md section 5. |
| Cut rooms 01, 08 and 09 until the content exists | The twelve-room sequence is the Phase 1 gate; each room still teaches the tech as the cheapest or most forgiving route and carries its control fixtures. |

## Feel reason

Movement: a tech that is the cheapest route and sometimes the only one is learned by feel, not by instruction. A room that falsely claimed to require a tech would punish players who found a legitimate faster line; one that is honest lets them. The rooms with a heart at stake (08) or a long fall (03, 07, 11) keep the stakes, so the tech is still the answer worth reaching for.

## Consequences

- When the boss (08), the shielded enemy (09), Snuffed (03, 12) and the weak-enemy pack (01) exist, revisit those rooms, turn the known-gap fixtures into controls, and update their headers.
- Known-gap and bypass fixtures (`08_gap_two_dashes`, `09_note_plain_jump_crosses`, `04_bypass_toss_wait`, `05_wall_jump_dash_cycle`) record costs; a change that closes or widens one is seen. The 10-bounce bound and the coyote lip rest on searches, not proofs.
- Director questions the fixers raised:
  1. Accept "taught, not enforced" for rooms 01, 08, 09, 04 until the content exists, or add a core rule? (Room 08's two-dash gap; the toss wait in room 04; room 05's wall-jump cycle.)
  2. Reverse pogo without a dash works because a bounce keeps full horizontal speed (BUILD.md); keep that as a second way or make the bounce keep less?
  3. Room 11: reset the toss cooldown on send-back, or show a HUD readout sooner, since a toss spent in the dip or on a failed throw locks the chasm for 12 s? Does "teleport after landing" in BUILD.md tech 11 mean the literal reading, or the mid-air teleport the room teaches (ADR-0017)?
  4. Room 03: is the main pit's chain (8 bounces at the run pace, a 10-frame window, a ladder up to the optional tunnel) the relaxed lineup you asked for, and is the 4-a-second tunnel with its Votive marker the right home for the checklist's line? Is the 72 tick margin in room 01 enough?
  5. Room 07: the window is narrow (6 to 10 plans per phase at the lip); play it. The Ember Toss is unsearched there.
  6. Room 10: the landing after a second dash is 51 u short of the pit (5 to 6 ticks of held run); lengthen? A toss from B's face at tick 12 crouches Ash into the wall again, easy to mistime.
  7. Solid dummies and the ambush `trigger` are core options; `Simulation.VERSION` was not bumped, since no existing behaviour changed. Bump and re-bless the goldens?
  8. Reviewer sign-off: some fixers could not spawn a fresh reviewer; rooms 10 and 11 were not re-reviewed independently.
