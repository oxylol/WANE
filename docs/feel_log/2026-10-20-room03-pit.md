## Why

The pit is sized from the chain the line names: first bounce 195.5 u inside the tunnel (x 771.5), 300 u a bounce at 4.0 a second, the tenth arc ending 187 u past the end (3584 u) and the ninth falling at x 3451, 133 u short. The route (`zone1_03_pogo_chain_route`) is a down-strike on each fall and an air jump 5 frames after each bounce, which holds each link to 15 frames; it counts 10 bounces, one every 15 frames, vx 20 u/f at every bounce, no hazard, out through the east door. A measured scan of strides 13 to 16 found the plain chain is exactly 13 (a later strike meets the spike first), an air jump alone gives 14 or 15, and nothing longer without an air dash.

What is possible besides, measured and pinned: a faster rhythm is not shorter (the plain chain covers 260 u a bounce and takes 11); a slower one is (an air dash and air dash-jump between bounces make a 520 u link: 6 bounces at 2.3 a second cross, 5 do not); and a slow last arc lets nine 4-a-second bounces cross (`03_route_slow_finisher`), as a slow first link would. So "fewer than 10 falls short" holds for a chain no link of which is slower than 15 frames, not for every chain: the line counts bounces at 4 a second, and a player who accepts a slow link buys 220 u with it. The geometry cannot forbid that without taking the air dash and the air jump away. `tests/unit/tools/test_zone1_room03_pit.gd` holds the room to the arithmetic.

Contact sheet, read row by row: row 1 shows Ash holding the middle of the view through ten evenly spaced arcs (the orange chart: ten equal arcs, then the run out along the east hall; the camera never takes control, the blue track follows at the lead); row 2 is the same nine arcs and then the fall into the spikes and the send-back to the hall (the chart's second segment at the right), 133 u short of the end; row 3 shows eleven narrower arcs of the plain chain. The red spike line runs under all of them at the same height as the hall floor, so each bounce reads as a hop off a hazard. At sheet size the player is a small box; the arcs and the rhythm are what the sheet judges, and they are even.

## How it plays now

A crossing is 221 ticks (3.7 s, 10 bounces), down from 343 ticks (5.7 s, 20 bounces) with the plain chain: the pit is still a rhythm to hold, now one a player can keep for 2.5 s rather than 5. The 4-a-second rhythm needs the air jump on every link, which makes the line harder to hit than the plain chain but is the chain the checklist names.
