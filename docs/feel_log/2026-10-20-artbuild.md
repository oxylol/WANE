## Why

What the build does: SVG parts in, three RGBA8 atlases per scale (shape: silhouette SDF, visible-edge distance, front-side flag, emissive; normal: bevel normal and gloss; parts: palette slot, brush angle, taper, flags), a palette image and a sheet JSON out, all evaluated exactly in float64 at each scale's pixel centres.

What the contact sheet shows, and what I wrote down looking at it:

- **Candle stump (the pipeline's test prop), channels.** The first design failed silhouette-first review: its drip lay inside the body, so it was interior detail, and the wide pool made the whole read as a top hat. Redesigned: the drip now runs out over the edge and ends in a bulb, the pool is narrower and lumpy. At 64 px it reads as a candle on a puddle, with the flame and wick as a spike on top. The U channel shows the seam where the stump overlaps the pool (a drawn line, as the ADR wants) and no line between the stump and its own drip (one part).
- **Candle, mock lit at Dusk and Pitch.** The mock is a few lines of numpy that stands in for the ADR 2 shader, only to judge the baked data; it is not the game's look. The flame is the only thing that holds at Pitch, the wax body drops to its shadow value, and the ink stays black on the shadow side. The bevel normals rake the lip and the bulb of the drip as intended.
- **Probe, smear frame.** A smear is the union of the blade at the sub-poses of its arc. Three sub-poses 20 degrees apart first baked as three separate spikes, a fan of copies and not a ribbon; the baker now adds poses between the animator's keys (polar interpolation about the part's pivot) until no vertex jumps more than 0.5 u, so a few keys sweep a continuous region. The test figure's swing sweeps 55 degrees and reads as a fan, which is right for the data given. **For the animator (task 2): author the smear arc as the trailing 10 to 15 degrees, not the whole swing, or the ribbon is a paper fan.** The taper channel first came out near zero in the wedge because the ray ran into the sub-poses' interior outlines; it now ray-casts against the union's own outline only.
- The normal map's tilt is 62 degrees at the edge, falling linearly to flat at 3 u inside. On the head circle it reads as a rounded slab.
- An unseen risk the first decode found: Godot's default texture import rewrites the colour of zero-alpha texels, and almost every texel of the shape and normal textures has zero alpha. `project.godot` now sets the importer defaults (no alpha-border fix, lossless, no mipmaps), and `tests/unit/art/test_art_import.gd` proves the imported textures equal their PNGs.

Measured: a 4x frame of the probe bakes in 0.33 s (the spike: 6.6 s; the ADR gate: 1.0 s). The candle sheet (7 frames, 1x, 2x and 4x) builds in about 3 s. A synthetic 40-part 144 u frame at all three scales takes 3.4 s, which sizes Ash's build (cached by frame, parallel with `--jobs`). The build is byte-identical serially, in a process pool and from the cache; the committed output equals a fresh build.
