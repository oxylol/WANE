## Why

Tech 8 has no teacher yet, so the room is three drops down a staircase, each ending in a one-hit dummy 76 u past the landing. The first dummy does not attack. The other two swing an unparryable (red) swing timed to land the tick after the landing of the teaching route, so the opening is one frame wide: a strike pressed 4 f early is live for the 2 last airborne and 2 first grounded frames, kills on the landing frame and goes on to its frame 14 (drop 1 at frame 63, drop 2 at 126, drop 3 at 182; hitstop moves the strike's later frames). The contact sheet shows the staircase, the camera following down it with no stop, and the dummies as boxes with their state labels; the player never slows between drops. Critique against section 5: the geometry reads at a glance (three equal steps), the dummies stand where the drop lands, and nothing but the strike's timing differs between route and controls. Panels are small at the film's default size; the plot at the end shows the run's x and y as two steady lines.

A dummy is no wall, so this cannot be the only way across (the header comment says so): a player can wait out a swing or dash through it. The controls show the cost: no strike loses a heart to the second swing (frame 127); a strike pressed on the landing frame is live 2 f later, the swing lands first, a heart goes and the strike is broken. A white swing would be parried by a press near it, which is why the swings are red.

## How it plays now

Run, step off the ledge, press attack just before the floor: the dummy dies as Ash lands and the swing goes on to its end with no landing lag; three drops in 210 f (3.5 s) with 5 hearts left, crossing into room 9. Proven by `08_strike_on_the_way_down` (never hearts below 5; strike frame 5 on each landing frame; room event at frame 210), and the two controls.
