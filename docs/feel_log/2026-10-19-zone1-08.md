## Why

Tech 8 has no teacher yet, so the room is three drops down a staircase, each ending in a one-hit dummy 76 u past the landing. The first dummy does not attack. The other two swing an unparryable (red) swing that lands on the tick after the teaching route's landing, so the opening is one frame wide for a strike pressed on the landing: a strike pressed 4 f early is live for the 2 last airborne and 2 first grounded frames, kills on the landing frame and goes on to its frame 14 (drop 1 at frame 63, drop 2 at 126, drop 3 at 182; hitstop moves the strike's later frames). The contact sheet shows the strike box live across the landing and the dummy dead before the swing, against the control where the swing lands first.

Review fix (F1, F3): the first version started the swings by the clock (period 104 and 160). The kill on dummy 1 freezes the world for 3 f, so the teaching route landed on 126 but a run that skipped dummy 1 landed on 123 against the same swing on 127, and a plain land-then-strike crossed with 5 hearts and 3 f faster. The swings now open on position (`trigger=284`, the ledge): the swing is 23 f after Ash reaches the ledge and 1 f after her landing whatever she did before, so a strike pressed on the landing loses a heart on either timeline (`08_control_land_then_strike`, landing 123, swing 124; `08_control_strike_after_landing`, landing 126, swing 127). The same change closes waiting (F2): a start delayed by 40 f is hit at the same place (`08_control_wait`), and one dash is hit at the third ledge (`08_control_one_dash`).

What is still open (F2): dashing. A dash (20 u/f) outruns a 24 f telegraph; two dashes (frames 100 and 132) cross with 5 hearts and no kill in 174 f, 36 f faster than the teaching route. A dummy is no wall and Phase 1 has no boss that blocks a door, so tech 8 is taught here and not enforced. `08_gap_two_dashes` pins the hole; the earlier claims that the teaching route is the only or the clearly best way were wrong and are removed. The opening is not one frame wide either: it is the 2 f between a landing strike's press and the swing, and a press on the landing is what loses.

## How it plays now

Run, step off the ledge, press attack just before the floor: the dummy dies as Ash lands and the swing goes on to its end with no landing lag; three drops in 210 f (3.5 s) with 5 hearts left, crossing into room 9. Proven by `08_strike_on_the_way_down` (never hearts below 5; strike frame 5 on each landing frame; room event at frame 210), and the controls.
