## Why

Each room makes its tech the cheapest way across, proved by control fixtures that try the strongest single-use alternatives (docs/adr/0016: it is not the only way, because the Ember Toss's cooldown can be waited out on a post and a wall-jump gives back the dash). The alternatives that matter here are vertical, and the Ember Toss is the strongest of them: its ember flies 400 u and then waits 120 ticks, so a player can throw it straight up from the top of a climb, fall back to the floor (the landing gives back the air jump and the air dash, and makes the next cling fresh), teleport up to it and climb on with everything restored. Every room's numbers are set against that trick, not just against the jump, air jump and dash.

## How it plays now

### Room 4, pogo-dash reset (the hidden room)

The main route is the floor, west door to east door, plain. Over its east half a tower of four floating spike posts (Phase 1's stand-in for BUILD.md's pogo-able spikes; a bell would be a solid to stand on) rises to an alcove in the east wall holding the hidden room's Lantern-tier Votive (an entity marker: Phase 1 has no pickups, and the room view does not draw markers). Post 1, 8 tiles up, is the safe first encounter: a jump and the air jump and a down-strike onto it, a free bounce over the floor. Each step after is 448 u: the bounce gives back the air jump and the air dash, and only the air jump plus the up-dash reach the next post. Post 1 to 2 spends the dash; posts 2, 3 and 4 each need the one the last bounce gave back (pogo, dash, pogo: the reference hidden-room gap). From the alcove a hole drops down a one-tile chute through the east door. Every wall is smooth.

- `04_hidden_route` (550 ticks): from the west door's arrival point, dashing along the floor, the four pogos (each restoring the spent air dash: `air_dashes` 0 the tick before, 1 on the bounce), the four up-dashes, the alcove's floor (2048 u up) on tick 450, the east door in the chute's fall on tick 548. The hidden route takes 9.2 s with the alcove; the plain route along the floor about 3.4 s.
- `04_control_no_second_dash` (318): pogo, dash, pogo and then no dash: the top is 965 u up, 155 u under post 3's reach. The natural try fails.
- `04_control_no_reset_toss` (430): the strongest climb that never uses a dash a bounce gave back: the one dash between posts 1 and 2, the ember thrown from the top after post 2 (960 u up), the fall to the floor, the teleport (1345 u up), the air jump and the dash the landing gave back, post 4: the top is 1859 u up, 179 u under the alcove's floor with the 10 u ledge-grab. With three posts the trick would reach the alcove; that is why there are four.
- `bypass/04_bypass_toss_wait` (2526, a bypass record, not a teaching route; the review's finding F1): with the toss's 12 s cooldown waited out by bouncing on a post, four tosses climb the tower and put the player on the alcove's floor on tick 2515 (42 s, against the hidden route's 450), with no air dash after the approach's three ground dashes (tick 150) and no hazard. Per post: a bounce, the air jump at its top, a drift 123 u away from the post, the ember thrown up and across 283 u short of the next post's column at the air jump's top, a fall back onto the post, the teleport 46 ticks after the throw, bounces until the toss is back (720 ticks). The room's header and ADR-0016 say so; the fixture pins its cost (never before tick 2400).

### Room 5, wall-kick climb (the well)

A well 384 u wide whose one clingable surface is its east wall (drawn blue-grey; the smooth walls are brown); the top of that wall is the exit floor, 2176 u up. The room is 36 tiles wide: the camera centres a room narrower than the view.

- `05_kick_climb` (413): the jump clings at its top, then a kick on each cling: 900 u/s up, 240 u/s away, 175.6 u every 24 ticks, twelve kicks, each cling left the tick after it starts (`wall_frames` never above 1: never stuck). Over the top on tick 386, the east door on 411 (6.9 s).
- `05_control_no_kick` (330): never a kick or a wall-jump: the jump, air jump and up-dash, the first cling since the floor (it gives back the air jump and dash), an up-dash straight from the cling and the air jump pressed while still rising (so it is no kick), the ember thrown from the top (914 u up), the fall, the teleport (1298 u up), everything again with a second fresh cling: the top is 2048 u up, 128 u under the exit floor. Away from the wall the same climb tops out at 1313 u.
- `05_wall_jump_dash_cycle` (334): not a control but the director question below, pinned: from every cling a wall-jump away and an up-dash on the next tick climbs the same wall 263 u every 25 ticks, over the top on tick 301, 85 ticks before the kicks.

## Open questions for the director

- **Kicks are not the best way up a single wall.** BUILD.md's wall-jump row ("Resets double jump and air dash") with ADR-0010's fresh cling (the first cling since a wall-jump restores both) lets a wall-jump and an up-dash on the next tick climb one wall at 10.5 u a tick (263 u every 25 ticks), against the neutral kicks' 7.3 (175.6 u every 24). No well can make kicks the only way up: a hazard that catches a wall-jump (8 u off the wall on its first tick) also catches a kick (9.3 u off the wall on its fourth tick), and a wall-jump followed at once by an up-dash stays 8 u from the wall. Play `05_kick_climb` and `05_wall_jump_dash_cycle` side by side. **Decided (docs/adr/0016, review finding F2):** accepted, no core change. The wall-jump's own restore feeds the cycle, so the options (restore on the next cling only; nothing on a re-cling) would change BUILD.md's wall-jump row and tech 6 for a room whose checklist line, a single wall climbed without feeling stuck, already holds. Kicks are the climb that spends nothing; the cycle is tech 6's rhythm, which needs wall-jump and dash put together and is taught in room 6. `05_control_no_kick` proves only that the kickless, wall-jump-less climb falls short, not that kicks are required.
- **The Ember Toss's landing trick sets every vertical room's size.** Throwing the ember up, falling to the floor and teleporting up to it with everything the landing gave back is worth about 400 u plus a fresh airtime. Rooms 4 and 5 are 76 and 78 tiles tall largely because of it (without it room 4 would need three posts, room 5 a well of about 1400 u). Is that the toss you want? A teleport that keeps the airtime it was thrown in (no restores from a landing in between), or an ember that goes out when the player lands, would let vertical rooms be shorter.
- **Room 4's alcove is reached only through tech, but the Votive marker is invisible.** The room view does not draw entity markers; the alcove reads as an empty recess until pickups exist.
- **Room 6's crossings use the air jump too.** At 640 u the dash alone (wall-jump and dash, no air jump) reaches 170 u, barely more than no dash at all (155 u); the crossing that works is wall-jump, dash, air jump, cling. A narrower shaft lets the wall-jump and air jump cross without any dash (297 u at 448 u). Is "wall-jump, dash, air jump, cling" the rhythm you want tech 6 to teach, or should the dash do more of the crossing on its own?
- **Times.** Room 4's plain route is about 3.4 s; its hidden route with the alcove 9.2 s. Room 5 is 6.9 s, room 6 8.1 s (both include about 2 s of running to and from the climb).
