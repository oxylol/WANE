## Change

First implementation; no number was tuned. A door's strip is 2 tiles deep (`RoomData.DOOR_DEPTH_TILES`): part of the room format's geometry, like the tile size, not a feel number (ADR-0014 says why it must be at least the standing body's height).

## Why

Task 7's zone is twelve rooms joined end to end. Crossing a room must cost the player nothing: no pause, no lost speed, no dropped input. Doors are crossed on the tick the body is wholly inside the opening's strip, so a run, a dash, a fall or a jump goes straight through with its speed, and the camera cuts on that tick.

## How it plays now

- Through a door Ash keeps the speed, the state and the action under way: a run arrives running (`door_walk_through_keeps_the_run`), a dash goes on counting its frames (`door_dash_carries_through`), a fall and a jump keep their vertical speed (`door_vertical_fall_and_jump`).
- Walking straight back returns Ash to just inside the door they came through (`door_walk_back_returns_through_the_same_door`).
- A room's enemies stay behind and stand again on the way back in (`door_enemies_belong_to_their_room`); a kill ember in flight reaches Ash as they go (`door_kill_ember_in_flight_reaches_ash`); an Ember Toss ember stays behind and goes out (`door_ember_stays_behind`).
- Spikes after a door send Ash back to safe ground in the new room (`door_hazard_after_a_door_stays_in_the_room`).

## Open questions for the director

- **The cut.** The camera cuts on the crossing tick, with no fade: nothing ever takes control. Play through a door at a run and at a dash and say whether the cut reads, or whether a few frames of fade over the moving player would read better.
- **Enemies reset when a room is left.** Phase 1 has only dummies; when zones have real enemies, should a room remember its dead until the zone is left?
