## Why: each finding, the measurement and the picture

**F3, the ember was a flat disc.** `ember_before_after.png`. Before, on the probe at Pitch through the post pass: the disc's luminance 0.74 to 0.75 from its centre to 2.2 u out, one whiteness (b/r 0.42) across it, then a 0.10 step at its edge; at 1x a 4 x 4 block of 0.744 (13 pixels within 0.02 of the hottest). After: 0.85 at the core grading 0.84, 0.83, 0.80, 0.76, 0.72, 0.69, 0.67, 0.65 a quarter u at a time into the robe and its halo (no adjacent step over 0.045, and under 0.04 across the outline on its lit side); b/r 0.67 at the core against 0.25 to 0.37 at the rim (orange-white to orange); at 1x two pixels at 0.81 with the ring around them at 0.59 to 0.69. The cause was `ink_shade` replacing the fill under an emissive with one colour; the heat now rises from the part's outline, so the same code draws Ash's ember, an eye or a flame. The review also stood the carrier's light 9 u above the probe's ember (at a share of its height); it now stands at the sheet's `ember` region, so the halo is centred on the core.

**F5, the background's line was closed at 4x.** `background_line_4x_before_after.png`, `background_line_1x_before_after.png`. The ledge, Dusk, with the review's ember (columns inked at a quarter of the heavy line's strength): its top edge 400 of 417 columns at 4x and 87 of 105 at 1x before; 10 of 417 and 3 of 105 after. Its underside 373 of 417 and 68 of 105 before; 214 of 417 (51%) and 53 of 105 (50%) after: the same line at both scales. The lit side drew a 0.3 u hairline, which at 1x falls under the 0.8 px floor and is drawn at 37%, but at 4x is a whole line; the breaks drew 35% of the weight. Now the side facing the light has no line, the breaks are gaps and the light's drop-out takes the weight. Making the weight whole at 4x showed a fault under it: on the pixels right on an edge the gradient of U (a crease there) turned the edge's normal over, so a floor's lit top edge drew black dashes at 4x (20 of 321 columns). The normal is now the gradient of U signed by its side, for every sprite: 0 columns.

**F6, the lens on every floor near the ember.** `floor_lip_before_after.png`, `room01_pitch_before_after.png`, `light_pools_before_after.png`. Before, the probe's floor lip at Pitch: the depth at which the lit skin falls to half was 4.25, 6.5, 7.5, 7.25 and 4.5 u at 60 u left of the ember to 60 u right (a lens), with a step of 0.21 of its light between two pixels (terraces); the twin's own test floor: 7.8, 4.6, 2.6, 1.8 and 1.4 u from under the light to 160 u away, steps of 0.107 between points 0.25 u apart. After: one depth along the floor (within 1.5 u at 4x on the screen, 0.5 u on the twin) and no step (under 0.08 on the screen, under 0.01 on the twin). Cause: the walk toward the light through the wall, which lengthened with the angle and stepped with its 1.5 u steps. Now the skin is the field's depth through the nearest face. A light 10 u above a floor (a Gutter Ember, or the ember of a Wick that crouches): before, every point in its sight saw 0.802 of it and the pool was drawn over with contour lines (steps of 0.198); after, 0.99 or more everywhere, no step over 0.01. Cause: its 14 u penumbra disc reached into the floor, and the march's last sample fell short of the light at a place that jumped from pixel to pixel. Now the penumbra radius is held within the light's clearance.

**F7, the smear was a V, not a ribbon.** `smear_swing1_before_after.png` (the probe's swing frame 1: 4x lit at Dusk, its alpha, 1x enlarged 4 times), `smear_age_channel_before_after.png`. Before: the age channel was the nearest edge's, so the half of the fan nearest the tail edge had age 0 (empty) and the half nearest the blade 1; along the arc at 14 u out the alpha jumped 0 to 0.85 across the fan's bisector; the anti-aliased fringe read the smear flag as off and drew opaque ink (1.00 beside the tail, a radial hairline across the motion: 49 dark pixels on the tail edge). After: the age is where along the arc the part last passed each pixel; the alpha at 10, 14 and 18 u out rises smoothly from 0.03 to 0.07 at the tail to 0.78 to 0.84 at the blade (no fall, no jump over 0.15); nothing beside the tail over 0.07; the tail edge carries no line (0 dark pixels against 294 on the blade's leading edge); the outer edge's line thins out toward the tail (0 dark pixels there, 20 at the blade). `pose.json` now refuses a smear arc on a part not tagged `smear` (it baked as a doubled solid part). The old check sampled at angles where the fan was already empty and passed on the V; the new ones measure the fill along three arcs, the specks, the outer line and the tail edge, and four of them failed on the old build.

**The key at Pitch.** Beyond every light at Pitch a surface showed 0.16 of its lit value (the key's 0.5 times the Dark's 0.15, through the gate 0.05 to 0.15); section 8 says only the ember and Beacons cast light at Pitch. The key's share now falls from 1 at Dusk to 0 at the Dark's floor: 0 at Pitch, 0.63 at Gloam's start, and Dusk is unchanged (shader mode 5 reads within 0.03 of 1 at Dusk and of 0 at Pitch). The ambient keeps its 0.15.

**The ember's numbers on the screen.** `content/lighting.tres` holds the spec's 420 u, 2400 K, 0.5 Hz and 4%. The GPU check now reads them off a render against BUILD.md's own numbers: the light on a flat grey surface reaches 420.0 u (from its strength at 150 and 330 u), adds 0.00000 at 430 u, has g/r 0.608 and b/r 0.237 (a 2400 K black body: 0.608, 0.237), and its crest at 0.5 s comes round at 2.5 s (0.07051 both, 0.06509 at the trough between: 1.083, the spec's 1.04 / 0.96). A tuning of 400 u and 2600 K failed it (the shading-maths check, which reads the table's own radius, had passed it).

## Critique of the pictures (BUILD.md section 10)

- The ember (`ember_before_after.png`, `probe/detail4x.png`): a white-hot point with an orange glow around it that carries on into the halo over the robe. At 1x it is one or two pale pixels on an orange smudge: it reads as a point of light, the player's cursor, not a sticker. The halo is now centred on it. The robe still meets the ember on its shadow side with a slightly darker crescent 2.5 u to the right: that is the robe's own two values, not the ember.
- The floors (`floor_lip_before_after.png`, `room01_pitch_before_after.png`): the lip is an even band that fades with depth and with distance from the light, the same on both sides of the ember; no lens and no terraces at 1x or 4x. A wall corner whose one face sees the light and the other does not now meets at a mitre along the corner's diagonal, which at the room field's 4 u texel steps a little (`light_pools_before_after.png`, the pillar's top left); at 1x it is a few pixels inside a wall.
- A light's pool (`light_pools_before_after.png`): smooth from the light to its reach, the pillar's shadow edge soft. The Gutter Ember lights only above itself, as it should.
- The background (`background_line_4x_before_after.png`): the ledge is drawn by its values and its shadow-side line; the top edge and the pillars' lit faces have none, the underside's line breaks off where the ember reaches it. At 1x and 4x the line is the same line. Painted edges that meet with no line are a crisp texel edge (the parts texture is nearest-sampled), which reads as a cut shape at 1x; magnified it is a hard pixel edge.
- The smear (`smear_swing1_before_after.png`): the blade now trails a fan that fades smoothly toward where it came from, with its ink only on the leading edge and the outer arc near the blade. It reads as speed. It is still a soft fill rather than the dry-brush streaks a painter would use, and the blade itself is at the ribbon's 0.85 opacity on that frame; both are for Ash's swings (task 2), not the channel. Magnified from the 1x sheet the age shows in texel steps (nearest sampling); at 1x it is one pixel a step.

## How it plays now

Nothing the player holds changed. The Wick's ember is a hot point with a glow; a floor under it is lit evenly along its lip; a light set low in a gutter fills its pool smoothly; at Pitch the world beyond the ember's reach is the ambient's alone, with no lit side from the room; backgrounds carry their ink only on the side away from the light; a swing's smear fades along its arc.
