## Change

The twelve rooms were built on the engine before the movement and combat fixes (side 1's 8-frame recoil, startup armor, landing actions ending knockback, buffered landing jumps, 20 free frames of invulnerability, the physics lip fix) and the procs. On the merged engine all but four of the 60 room fixtures held (two in room 01, two in room 08); all four failed for one reason: side 1's recoil slows the run 1 u a frame while it lasts, so a route that strikes arrives later than it did.

- Room 01: every slash's recoil costs the run 1 u a frame for 9 frames; eleven slashes cost the control five ticks and the route one. Both fixtures are re-timed; the margin grows by 4 ticks.
- Room 08 (the finding): in the teaching route the strike on drop 1 costs 9 u, and the strike on drop 2 adds more, so Ash is 9 u (drop 2) and 16 u (drop 3) behind where she was, and the kill falls a frame after the landing, level with the swing's first active tick (the player's hit resolves first, so a strike pressed on the landing frame also won the tie). `08_control_strike_after_landing` therefore crossed: the room no longer gated drop 2. Dummy C is moved 9 u toward the ledge, which restores the route's kill on the landing frame (126) and the swing on 127; the control is hit again. Dummy D moves 9 u the other way with a 9 u longer trigger so that its kill again falls on the landing (184) and its swing on the tick after (185). The runs with no strike before them have no recoil: they are 9 u ahead of the teaching route and meet C's swing on the tick they land (123, not 124), D's a tick later in `08_control_one_dash` (165, not 164). They are hit all the same, so their expectations moved a frame and their points stand.
- The two-dash gap in room 08 moved with the room: dash 2 at 134 instead of 132 still crosses with 5 hearts and no kill (174 ticks). It is still a known gap, not a teaching route.
- A room's swing is anchored to where Ash is, not to the clock (room 08), so a one-frame shift of a route is a one-frame shift of the swing; rooms with such an ambusher must be re-timed against the recoil whenever the strike's recovery changes.

## The loop

`zone1_loop`: 3584 ticks (59.7 s) from room 01's west arrival with `option kindle 1` through the twelve rooms and out of room 12's east door into room 01, no hit taken. Per room, in ticks: 359, 242, 344, 552, 412, 487, 161, 211, 214, 179, 247, 176. Each room runs its own teaching route, started the tick after the previous door is crossed; Ash arrives with the speed she left with, which is 9 u a frame at most. Rooms 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12 take their routes unchanged (rooms 2, 8, 9 and 12 cross 2 or 3 ticks sooner on the carried speed, rooms 3, 5, 6 and 10 on the very tick). Room 4 needed 3 ticks of left first: the carried speed puts her 11 u ahead and the pogo posts' bounce landing spots with it; with no prefix she is hit by the spikes and sent back, and with 8 neutral ticks first she makes the alcove, 5 ticks later than the 3 of left; room 7 needs 2 ticks of left, and room 11 one. Over 600 frames by design (it is the one fixture that is). The fixture stage time is in `ci/test.sh --quiet`'s output.

## Why

Only the rooms' stand-in numbers moved; the movement and combat numbers are the fixes' own. The loop is the checklist's proof that the twelve rooms join: it fails when a door, a route or a carried-speed arrival breaks.
