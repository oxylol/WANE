## Change

No number was tuned. Three rules changed in the physics sweep; the 8 u lift, the walk's 9 u a tick and the dash's 20 u a tick are as they were.

| Rule | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A foot corner meeting a box's top corner exactly, walking down a ramp | read as the box's side: a 0 u step, refused | read as its top: Ash walks on along it |
| The ground and air corner lift | to the highest top anywhere under the box moved a sub-pixel | to the highest top just ahead of the leading edge |
| A solid box's top inside a pit or spike tile | loaded | refused at load |

## Why

Walking or dashing down a ramp, Ash stopped dead in two places and stayed stopped until a jump:

- where a whole-unit door or lip's top is exactly level with the feet as the leading edge reaches it (review 8, H1: 135 of 7,680 such joins);
- where a door's top is a few units above the feet while Ash's back foot is still over the plateau at the ramp's top. On a 45-degree ramp that back corner sits up to 10 u inside the plateau's wall, and the lift counted that wall: a door 5 u proud measured 9 u, past the 8 u nudge. This one predates H1's fix; the corner fuzz found it once H1 no longer stopped the walk first. Where the plateau measured within the 8 u, Ash was lifted to its height and popped up to 5 u above the door he stepped onto.

And a room could load with a box laid low in a pit or in spikes, so standing on solid ground cost a heart (L1).

## How it plays now

- Down a 45-degree or 1:2 ramp onto a door at its foot or a lip on it, Ash steps on and runs on at full speed, walking and dashing; no frame of the walk stops against a corner he visibly cleared (the six ramp fixtures, `never hit_wall`).
- A door 5 u proud met with the back foot still on the plateau is a 5 u step, the same as anywhere else (`walk_down_ramp45_onto_crest_lip`: on the door on frame 15 and along it at 9 u a tick to the room's edge; the dash at 20 u a tick).
- A lid over a pit is ground only when laid level with the pit's top or above it; one laid lower is refused when the room loads, and the flush lid is crossed unhurt (`walk_over_flush_pit_lid`).
