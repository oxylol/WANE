## Why

The tech is "dash into wax with the Votive". The room gives a safe first meeting (a one-tile screen in the open, x 704..736, floor to ceiling, nothing to fall into) and then the real use (a two-tile wax door, x 1280..1344, on the far lip of a spike chasm x 1088..1280). Both run floor to ceiling, so without Kindle nothing goes over or under: the door is the only way, proved by controls (the dash stops at 694 u; a jump, air dash and air jump over the chasm cling to the wax at 1270 u and the fall ends on the spikes). The sheet shows the screen's column lit then gone after frame 63, the dash through the door in the air above the spikes (frames 109 to 117), and Ash landing past it at frame 131.

## How it plays now

About 3 s from the west arrival (177 frames): run, dash into the screen at 147 u (the dash is 180 u, so it must start within 180 u of the face), run, jump at the lip, dash at the apex, land beyond the door, run to the east door. Crossing keeps the run into room 1 (x 74 u). The air dash is the only thing that gets through: the jump alone clings to the wax.
