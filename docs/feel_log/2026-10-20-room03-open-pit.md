## Why

The pit is sized by what stops a route without a pogo. With a roof there the roof stopped them (ceiling 64 u: 672 u in); with open air above, only width does. `tools/replay/gap_reach.gd` (new options `--room`, `--edge`, `--ledge`; defaults unchanged) on the new `test/longpit`, the pit with no far side, finds 2432 u past the edge for the dash-jump with the air jump, the air dash and the Ember Toss, 2243 u without the toss, 1333 u for a carried jump and 1201 u for a running jump. A crossing needs more than the width less half a body, so 2496 u leaves the strongest route 54 u short (the room 02 chasm leaves 62 u). That fixes the length of the chain, not its pace: 8 bounces at the run pace, fewer at a dash-jump's speed. It is what the director's complaint leaves open, and the honest answer to "a few bounces, not a gauntlet": a pit that no kit crosses without the pogo cannot be much shorter, and the bounces in it are relaxed. A roof low enough to shorten it is what he rejected. The ladder to the optional path stands 320 u west of the edge so that no platform is a launch pad for the pit; the unit test holds that, and holds the controls to the room.

The entry lines itself up: the first 14 tiles (448 u) are 128 u deep (8 at first; see the review follow-up below), so a run off the edge has 17 frames before the spikes and the down-strike window is 10 frames. The spike line then rises to 64 u under the floor, and its last 4 tiles to 32 u, so the last bounce has a lip to land past. Robustness, measured with a bot that strikes when the fall is T frames from the spikes: T from 4 to 13 all cross (2 and 3 and 14 and over miss), and six start points from 60 to 210 u (hops at different places) cross for T at 4, 8 and 12: the pit's rhythm does not depend on where the run began. A missed bounce costs a heart and the run back from where Ash last stood (x 199 u, `03_main_control_missed_bounce`).

## How it plays now

Run east, hop the patch (or bounce on it once), run off the hall's edge, press a down-strike when the spikes are close (a 10-frame window) and keep going: 8 bounces, about 5 s, onto the east hall and the door. A miss costs a heart. A player who wants the 4-a-second chain climbs the ladder in the hall's well, runs the upper hall, dash-jumps into the low tunnel and chains ten bounces at 4 a second (`zone1_03_pogo_chain_route`, unchanged), hops the shaft to the Lantern Votive marker (`03_optional_reward`) and drops back (`03_optional_way_back`). Missing the chain costs only a hazard. The shaft can also be climbed from below by the whole kit (a jump, the air jump and an up air dash lift the feet 404 u): the reward is no gate (ADR-0016).
