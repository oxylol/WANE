## Change

First implementation. The numbers are the doc's (the coefficients 1.0, 0.8, 0.5, 0.3, 0.2; the chain depth 3; the Flame tier's 10%; the Knuckle's 3, the Wick's 8%, the Bell's 10% and 120 u, the Ember's one dash, the Step's heart), now in `content/combat/procs.tres` and `content/votives/`. Two are new:

| Number | Before | After | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Bell of the Vigil's stun (`stun_frames`) | none (BUILD.md: "stunning everything in 120 u") | 30 | frames, the length of a poise break's stagger |
| A proc's grey-box mark (`content/votive_looks.tres`) | none | the Bell 9 f, 120 u (its reach); Second Ember 8 f, 20 u; test Votives 6 f, 28 u | frames, units |

And one reading of the doc: the Flame tier's "+10% chance" is relative (10% becomes 11%), as its "+10% damage" is.

## Why

Task 8's stress room needs 40 procs, and the game had none. Every choice above protects the hit: a Votive's damage is dealt but never adds hitstop (ten Knuckles make side 1 deal 40 and it still stops for a light hit's 2 frames), a proc's own hit freezes nothing and fills neither Kindling nor Flame, and a proc's mark is the least important thing on screen, so no hit spark or telegraph ever loses its cell to one. The sheet shows it: in the 39-proc burst only the hit's own cell and one other light up, and the dummy's state is readable through it.

## How it plays now

- Tallow Knuckle: side 1 deals 13 (`tallow_knuckle_adds_three`), the same 2 frames of hitstop with ten of them (`tallow_knuckle_never_adds_weight`), and the pogo too (`tallow_knuckle_on_the_pogo`); Kindle's 30 is untouched.
- Pilgrim's Wick: Ash runs at 583 u/s, reached in the same 6 frames, in the air too (`pilgrims_wick_runs_faster`, `pilgrims_wick_in_the_air`); the dash is unchanged.
- Bell of the Vigil: ten stacks ring on every Taper hit (no roll), fifteen ring twice half the time (`bell_fifteen_stacks_*`), a Kindle tick rings half as often (`bell_on_kindle_ticks_at_half_chance`), the Flame tier turns a 90% miss into a 99% ring (`bell_flame_tier_*`); a ring during a telegraph takes the swing away (`bell_stun_takes_the_swing`).
- Second Ember: a pogo off an enemy or the spikes leaves two air dashes, until Ash lands (`second_ember_*`).
- Hollow Step: the ground dash fires again the frame it is pressed after the first one's carry-over; the run starts on 4 hearts, never fewer than 1 (`hollow_step_*`).

## Open questions for the director

- **The Bell's stun.** 30 frames, and it cancels a swing even after its sound has played (a poise break does too). Play the sandbox's swinging dummies with ten Bells (`godot --path . -- --room=sandbox --votive=bell_of_the_vigil:10`; any Votive and stack count the same way) and say whether a stun that late reads as fair, and whether 30 f is long enough to feel like a ring.
- **Kindle's cone as a tick.** The cone's hit is Kindle's first proc event, then each burn tick; the doc's "Kindle 0.5 per tick" could also mean the burn ticks alone.
- **The Flame tier's bonus** read as relative.
- **Hollow Step's floor** of one heart.
- **The Abbess's Mercy** needs quarter hearts (ADR-0015): a change to the hearts and to the ember's cracks on the HUD. Should hearts carry quarters?
- **Hearthshard and the Snuffed Blade are deferred** (PROGRESS.md): the Hearthshard needs the light (Phase 2 task 1) and the Snuffed Blade needs the Dark's clock; neither can be built or judged in grey boxes, so Phase 1 ships five of the eight example Votives.
