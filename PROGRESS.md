- **Proc storms in late runs (a design question for Phase 2's Votives)**: the spec's "40 procs" is one Taper hit's chain, and the gated stress run is that. A Votive that chains on every proc event, from every source, with no cooldown, costs **about 22 us a proc** (34 before ADR-0020 Decision 17; measured on the storm run, `ci/perf.sh --only stress` prints it): one swing on a bunched crowd is 300 to 550 procs, 5 to 10 ms in one tick since the cells went native (12 to 17 before), a stutter on a 16.7 ms frame.
